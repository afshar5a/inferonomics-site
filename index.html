What Inferonomics helps you decide

Move from cost visibility to architecture decisions.

Most AI cost tools tell you what you already spent. Inferonomics is designed to help you understand what your architecture choices are likely to cost before you commit to them.

Compare architectures

Evaluate different AI system designs under the same demand assumptions and see how cost structure, retrieval strategy, and model choice affect viability.

Stress-test margins

Model how margins change as usage volume rises, prompts grow, retrieval depth increases, or cache performance deteriorates.

Find the break-even point

Identify when a workflow becomes sustainable, what moves the threshold, and which optimization levers matter most.

Example analysis

What happens when you add RAG to a support workflow?

A team is deciding whether to add retrieval to improve answer quality. Inferonomics helps model how deeper retrieval affects cost per usable output, margin resilience, and the break-even point as volume grows.

Questions the model can answer

  • Should we use RAG here, or does it destroy margins?
  • How sensitive is this workflow to retrieval depth?
  • Does caching materially improve viability?
  • Which lever matters most: routing, compression, or caching?
