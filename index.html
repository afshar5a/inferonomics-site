About Inferonomics

Inferonomics is an independent AI Economics Research Lab exploring how architectural decisions influence the long-term economic viability of AI systems.

The research focuses on unit economics, margin resilience, token efficiency, and cost-aware design principles that help organizations understand the structural sustainability of AI features.

About the Founder

Afshar Sanam is a technologist exploring the intersection of AI systems architecture, economic design, and financial modeling. Her work focuses on how architectural decisions shape cost structure, margin resilience, and the long-term sustainability of AI-powered systems.

Through Inferonomics, she examines unit economics as a first-class design constraint — developing frameworks that help leaders evaluate AI features not only for capability, but for economic viability.

This research initiative is developed independently and outside professional responsibilities. No confidential or employer information is used.
