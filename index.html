About Inferonomics

Inferonomics is an independent AI Economics Research Lab exploring how architectural decisions influence the long-term economic viability of AI systems.

The research focuses on unit economics, margin resilience, token efficiency, and cost-aware design principles that help organizations understand the structural sustainability of AI features.

About the Founder

Inferonomics was founded by Afshar Sanam, a Senior Engineering Leader building distributed systems and enterprise platforms. Her work centers on the intersection of AI systems, architectural decision-making, and economic design.

This initiative is developed independently, outside professional responsibilities, and does not use confidential information from any employer.
