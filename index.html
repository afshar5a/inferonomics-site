About Inferonomics

Inferonomics is an independent AI Economics Research Lab examining how architectural decisions influence cost structure, margin resilience, and long-term sustainability in AI systems.

The research treats economic viability as a first-class architectural constraint — ensuring AI capability is matched with structural financial durability.

About the Founder

Inferonomics was founded by Afshar Sanam, a technologist exploring the intersection of AI systems architecture, economic design, and financial modeling.

Her work focuses on how design decisions shape cost structure, margin behavior, and long-term system sustainability.

This research initiative is developed independently and outside professional responsibilities. No confidential or employer information is used.
